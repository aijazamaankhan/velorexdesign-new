Before you choose

Is every piece exactly identical?

Natural wood grain and handmade finishing can create small visual differences. Product dimensions and functional details should remain consistent with the listing.

How does checkout work?

The current store records your order request and then opens WhatsApp so availability, customization and payment can be confirmed directly.

Are these campaign images exact product photographs?

The editorial environments are AI-composited from Velorex product references. Original product photographs remain available in the working store for factual inspection.
